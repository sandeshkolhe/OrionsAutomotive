Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding our workshop facilities, Glasurit paint warranties, OEM testing confidentiality, and billing.

What makes BASF Glasurit waterborne paint better than conventional paints?

BASF Glasurit 90 Line is engineered with German waterborne technology that eliminates toxic solvent off-gassing, resulting in superior health safety and environmental protection. For your vehicle, it offers higher solid pigment density, uncompromised factory color-matching with digital spectrometry, and superior resistance to chipping and UV degradation under harsh Indian tropical conditions.

How do you handle OEM confidentiality for unreleased test prototypes?

Our Andheri East facility operates dedicated blackout bays with biometric access control. All technician and telemetry personnel operate under strict Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs). Prototype vehicles are housed under secure covers, monitored by 24/7 CCTV, and testing routes are geofenced with real-time encrypted data telemetry directly synchronized to OEM engineering hubs.

Can you restore and coat outdoor metal sculptures and historical artefacts?

Yes. Our 4th specialized pillar combines metallurgic science and artistic finishes. We work with cast bronze, stainless steel, corten steel, aluminum, and fiberglass. Using marine-grade anti-corrosive epoxy primers and museum-grade UV-inhibitor clear coats, we protect public monuments and private installations against oxidation, bird lime, and marine salt corrosion.

What is the typical turnaround time for full mechanical service or respray?

Standard periodic mechanical maintenance and diagnostics are completed same-day (4 to 8 hours). Full Glasurit body resprays typically take 4 to 6 business days to ensure meticulous surface sanding, primer baking, basecoat color application, and multi-stage clear-coat polishing in our Italian downdraft booth.
