The Four Disciplines of Orionis

Unwavering commitment to material science, zero-compromise mechanical rigor, and artistic surface preservation.

1. Mechanical Repairs, Maintenance & Services

Complete mechanical diagnostics, engine rebuilds, transmission overhauls, and Brembo brake skimming. Calibrated for German sports cars, luxury SUVs, and exotic collectors who refuse shortcuts.

  • Computerized CAN-Bus telemetry & ECU adaptation
  • 3D Laser Wheel Alignment & high-speed balancing
  • OEM-certified genuine spare parts with warranty

2. Paint Job (Glasurit Brand, Eco-Friendly Paint)

Utilizing Germany's celebrated BASF Glasurit 90 Line waterborne coating system. Zero orange-peel, showroom optical depth, and environmentally sustainable low-VOC formulation.

  • Digital spectrophotometer color matching for 250,000+ formulas
  • Italian dust-free heated downdraft baking oven (65°C)
  • 9H ceramic quartz & self-healing TPU PPF stone-chip armor

3. Specialised On-Duty Testing & Validation to OEMs

Rigorous proving-ground fleet endurance, NVH acoustic logging, and severe tropical thermal stress testing conducted for automotive manufacturers under ironclad Non-Disclosure Agreements.

  • Multi-axis accelerometer telemetry & strain-gauge data
  • Biometric blackout prototype bays ensuring zero leaks
  • ADAS camera & millimeter radar dynamic calibration

4. Special Paint Jobs for Artefacts & Sculptures

Artisanal coating technology transferred to monumental civic sculptures, bronze heritage figures, and gallery installations exposed to aggressive coastal weather and marine salt air.

  • Marine-grade anti-corrosion barrier primers
  • Museum-grade UV-absorber clear coats preventing sun fading
  • Custom candy translucent, chromatic pearl, and satin finishes
